WiFi Room Mapper

Walk room to room, measure, then place your extender where the score holds up.
Browsers can't read WiFi signal strength or the network name, so this measures what you actually experience: latency, jitter, packet loss and download/upload speed through your WiFi. Compare rooms on the same network (stay on the router's WiFi, or on the extender's, while testing). Speed scores are relative to the best result recorded. Results stay on this phone.
